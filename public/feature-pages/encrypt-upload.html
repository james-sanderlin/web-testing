Encrypt Then Upload

Reproduces the "read a local file, encrypt it in the browser, then POST the ciphertext to an API" pattern (the Chainguard CreateArgosDocument flow). The plaintext file never leaves the browser — only an opaque encrypted payload does. Pick a mode, choose a file, and watch the Outbound body panel to see exactly what a network proxy would observe.
